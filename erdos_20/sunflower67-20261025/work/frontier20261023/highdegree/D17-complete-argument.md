## Statement and direct scope

Let F be a finite simple family of exactly68 four-element sets, with
no three distinct members having equal pairwise intersections; empty
cores are forbidden. Assume every point has degree at most17 and an
actual point x has degree17. Under the named universal imports, the
argument below gives a contradiction.

The global degree cap is an explicit hypothesis. The proof does not
need the status of the degree18, degree16 or low-degree branches to
supply it. The complete link L_x consists of all17 triples obtained
from actual members through x, and the residual family F_x^c has51
members. The classification and the projection both use this complete
link, not a selected17-subfamily of a larger point-star.

The same conditional exclusion extends to |F|>=68 with cap17 and an
actual degree17 point: retain all17 members through that point and
extend them to a68-member subfamily. This preserves SF4, cap17 and
the complete degree17 link. Applying the exact68 statement to that
subfamily suffices. In particular, this is not an assertion that every
family of at least68 members has such a point.

## Ordinary terminal bound13+27 on arbitrary support

Once a P9 plan excludes all core four-sets and all mixed core-triple
traces, every residual member has core trace of size at most2 or a
pure triple in Y or Z. For an E3 exclusion, a member with a mixed
three-point core trace has exactly one exterior point; that actual
point may be relabelled to the canonical marker. All other exterior
points remain unrestricted. This supplies an exclusion of every
completion of the mixed triple, not an ambient support bound.

If residual members A,B are disjoint, their union must meet every
block in each fixed component. Otherwise the corresponding actual
root cone is disjoint from both, giving an empty-core sunflower.
P9 avoids any at-most-two-point Y set and each I8 component avoids
any at-most-one-point Z set. Hence their union has at least3 Y points
and at least2 Z points.

If neither member has a pure Y-triple trace, one must supply two Y
points and the other at least one. The first then has no Z point
and the second at most one, because mixed triples and core four-sets
are absent. This contradicts the required two Z points. Therefore
one member has a pure Y triple, and the other must supply at least
two Z points and no Y point. The pure Y triple itself meets every
P9 block.

Remove residuals whose pure Y trace is a transversal triple. The
remaining residual family is intersecting, hence has at most27
members by H27. The transversal triples are exactly the nine P9
blocks, the omitted P10 block B and its complement C. Each of the
nine blocks already occurs in a root cone and therefore has at most
one residual completion by triple codegree2. B and C each have at
most two. Thus at most9+2+2=13 members were removed, and

    |F_x^c| <=13+27=40<51.

The literal stage checker independently reconstructs these eleven
triples and their capacities. The proof needs no pure-Z-triple
exclusion and no two-point avoidance property for Q8. It is expanded
in `D17-generic-stage-plan.md` and
`D17-Q8-E3-terminal-support.md`.
