## Statement and size convention

Let F be a finite simple family of four-element sets, with no three distinct members having equal pairwise intersections. Empty sunflower cores are forbidden. Assume H27 and the universal/prior facts below. The degree-18 assertion assembled here is:

### Universal degree20 and restriction to exactly68

The conventional degree bound is explicitly universal. In the frozen primary source, `paper/main.tex:333–350`, Corollary `cor:triple20` states that a simple SF3 family has at most20 triples, and consequently an SF4 family satisfies

    d(v)≤20,   d(u,v)≤6,   d(u,v,w)≤2.

Its proof precedes the size69 reduction. For an actual triple A in an SF3 family, its exact singleton classes have size at most3 and exact pair classes at most1; hence |N[A]|≤1+3·3+3·1=13. A family of at least21 triples has at least8 members disjoint from A. Those members are intersecting, while B4 makes N[A] intersecting. The intersecting SF3 bound10 then gives total at most20, a contradiction. Passing from an SF4 point-link to F adds the same point to every member and preserves a forbidden sunflower, including the corresponding enlarged core. The pair and triple bounds similarly follow from SF2≤6 and SF1≤2. Thus this cap is independent of any μ42 or size69 computation.

Hereditary restriction is available when a source statement is stated only at size68. Given |F|≥68 and a specified point x of degree d≤20, retain **all** d members through x and any68−d other members. There are enough other members. The resulting F0 has68 members, retains d_F0(x)=d_F(x), and retains the entire link L_x unchanged. Simplicity, uniformity, sunflower-freeness, and an upper degree bound are preserved. In particular, one may apply the exact-size degree20 exclusion to such a subfamily for every hypothetical degree20 point, then the degree19 exclusion after the universal cap has decreased, then the present degree18 exclusion. Arbitrary deletion of members through x would not justify retaining its classified complete link; the prescribed selection avoids that issue.

The actual-anchor inequality follows anew in F or F0. For A∈F, the family disjoint from A is intersecting, since two disjoint such members together with A would form an empty-core sunflower. H27 therefore gives

    |N_F[A]|≥|F|−27≥41.

There is no transfer of a lower-degree or support assertion through deletion. Any such prior conclusion used by a model is derived under μ≥41 and its stated degree cap in the selected family itself.

### P10+I8 terminal bound37

All four-core and mixed-three traces have now been excluded by the active marker chain, conditional on its certificates. The surviving traces are of size≤2 or are pure triples in one component.
