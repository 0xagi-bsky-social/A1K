# Cap15 NO44: composition check with the exact-rho7 theorem

24 October 2026. Ordinary research development; no solver, catalogue
replay, or Lean job. The conclusion is conditional on the stated universal
prior facts and the finite I7 catalogue, at their existing assurance level.
It does not assume a classification of size15 links with rho at most6.

## Scope and dependencies

Let F be a finite simple SF4 family of size68, with empty-core sunflowers
forbidden, mu at least41 and maximum point degree at most15. Use H27,
B4, the I8 classification/isolation theorem, HP15 in its recorded domain,
and the preceding ordinary cap15 no-P9/no-P10 and NO45 deductions.
The P8 type having no two-cover is excluded by the already established
cap16 argument, whose weaker degree and neighbourhood hypotheses hold
here. These give singleton cap8 and N(Q) at most44 at every actual Q.

## Checks of the two potentially delicate joins

At a singleton7 root i of an N44 anchor R, the complete link L_i has
size15. Its actual triple R minus i has exactly seven disjoint members.
Thus rho(L_i) is at least7. The global singleton cap8 applies at every
actual anchor, so rho is either7 or8. This excludes rho at most6 by an
actual witness, not by an unproved classification.

If rho is8, choose an actual link triple B with eight disjoint members
H. The complete remainder K is an intersecting size7 family by B4.
For P8, isolation makes K avoid all of supp(H): any deleted-block
exception is disjoint from B and would belong to H, contrary to its
definition. For Q8, the identical argument isolates its five-point
core, since either exceptional hub-plus-both-leaves triple is also
disjoint from B. It deliberately does not isolate the two leaves.

Every actual block of the remaining two-cover P8 or Q8 contains a point
of internal degree5. The distinguished R minus i has all point degrees
at most4, so it belongs to K. P8 separation would give it eight disjoint
companions, impossible. In Q8 it contains exactly one far leaf; its
seven disjoint companions are precisely the other seven H blocks.
The one near H block is leaf-plus-two-hubs. The exact pair category
containing it has no second member: any second residue would avoid
both hubs by core isolation and be disjoint from the first residue,
giving a sunflower with R. The remaining two category sizes must be
3 and3, and their triangles agree by intersection of K. The exceptional
hub edge avoids that triangle. This verifies the precise (1,3,3)
exception used in the N44 reduction, without an I7 classification.

In profiles B,D no pair category of size1 is available. In C the unique
candidate edge would both belong to and avoid the common triangle of
the high roots. In A two exceptions would give the actual E=01ab with
pair degrees at least (2,5,5,5,5,3), triple degrees exactly (1,1,2,2),
and point-degree sum at most60. Hence N(E) at most40, contrary to mu41.
At least one low link therefore has rho exactly7 in every case.

The rho7 classification's one-uv case uses one implicit elementary
obstruction worth making explicit: with extras ubr and vbs, r different
from s, a K* triple b+e whose exterior edge avoids r,s would make a
sunflower with those two extras, with core b. This justifies its bound
on the exclusive b-star. All the other uses of a residual triangle
versus a fixed edge are matching-number-two obstructions in an actual
point-link. No step upgrades a contained family to a complete link.

## Conclusion and the remaining domain

R16 minus a hub triple has no degree below4. R16 minus an outer triple
has just two degree3 points, and their only remaining joint triple has
a degree6 hub as its third point. Therefore neither deletion type has
an actual triple with point-degree pattern (2,4,4) or (3,3,4).
The low-root witness supplied above is impossible. Consequently

    N(Q) <= 43 for every actual Q in the cap15 domain.

This verifies the stated NO44 composition. It does not exclude N43,
the entire cap15 branch, or arbitrary size15 links with rho at most6.
Those remain separate mathematical tasks.
