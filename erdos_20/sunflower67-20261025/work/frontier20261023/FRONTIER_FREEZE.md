## What the search actually supports

1. **Exactly four-uniform, three-petal maximum:** the strongest comparable
   primary-source claim located remains **54 ≤ M(4,3) ≤ 68**, Babanskyy,
   aiXiv:2610.01292v1, 9 October. Both it and Axante et al.'s arXiv:2609.06175
   still display only v1. Their checked companion commit identities are unchanged.
   No eligible improvement or correction was found in the bounded search.
   This does not independently validate the external proof chain.
2. **General ground-set problem:** Bucić, He and Lee's 19 October
   [arXiv:2610.26686v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26686v1) reports the
   Erdős–Szemerédi conjecture for all numbers of petals. Its Theorem 1 gives
   3(2−1/(16k³))^n. It is not the uniform Erdős–Rado conjecture and provides
   no direct improvement of the rank-four constant 68. Thus the October 9
   finite paper must not be called the latest frontier of the entire subject.
3. **Constructive ground-set capacity:** primary repository leads now report
   μ₃ ≥ (1127+√925536)^(1/17) = 1.5678015201… (xSyllaa, 20 October v2), beyond
   older transfer-chain claims. Its base-family checks have not been rerun in
   this literature phase. It concerns mixed-size families, not M(4,3).
4. **Other relevant methods:** the bounded-VC result, robust derandomization,
   faithful-support slice-rank obstruction, and moonflower theorem are distinct
   statements. Their parameter/definition restrictions must be respected.
   Full-conjecture titles with incomplete or unverified proofs are not accepted
   as established solutions merely because they occur on a preprint server.

## Research decision

The first-search requirement is satisfied as a documented, bounded refresh.
Substantive work may now begin. The main goal stays exclusion of 68 members.
Its first gap is the μ41 high-degree/bootstrap boundary; the Oct20 conditional
terminal extension cannot be promoted to a global theorem.

Parallel leaves may investigate the extremal intersecting-27 boundary, a
support-independent high-degree model, and rigorous constructive improvements
in the adjacent capacity problem. Structural results are useful intermediate
work, but must meet their own universal coverage obligations. An adjacent
numerical gain would be reported precisely, not automatically relabelled a
major breakthrough or a solution of the sunflower conjecture.

Final review and publication remain **closed**. No qualifying breakthrough has
been established at this freeze. Historical final-review rounds remain stopped.
