## October 19 development and its scope

Bucić, He and Lee, [arXiv:2610.26686v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26686v1), submitted 19 October 2026, state for every k≥3 and n≥1 that a k-sunflower-free family F⊆2^[n] has |F|≤3(2−1/(16k³))^n (Theorem 1). Their vector theorem is V_k(D,n)≤((2k)^(2/(k−1))D^(1−1/(k−1)))^n. The paper's Sections 1–5, including the interpolation, common-space dimension argument, counting and random-block transfer, were read. Section 5 explicitly leaves the uniform Erdős–Rado conjecture open and identifies O(k log r)^r as the current general bound. For k=3 its set-family estimate does not improve Naslund–Sawin's older base 3/2^(2/3); the vector exponent improves from 2/3 to 1/2. The authors report Lean verification; the public artifacts below provide stronger evidence than that sentence alone. No later arXiv version was displayed.

The distinction is mathematical: n above is ground-set cardinality, whereas rank r bounds each member's cardinality. A bound exponential in n is not a bound depending only on r. Substituting r=4 for n is invalid. Even the trivial support estimate n≤4|F| produces only |F|≤3(2−1/432)^(4|F|), which cannot force a useful absolute upper bound. Thus this is a substantial post–October 9 theoretical development, but gives no direct numerical improvement for M(4,3).

Potential mechanism reuse is a research lead: for a genuinely multipartite encoding, a rational detector supported on constant or pairwise-distinct coordinate tuples can be expanded into a common low-degree function space. To use this on arbitrary rank-4 families one still needs a loss-controlled representation/transfer that removes ambient-alphabet dependence. This missing bridge is not supplied by the new theorem. Treat this as a proposed route, not a derived bound.

## Other claims and revisions

- [Mishra, 2606.02667v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02667v2), June 8, is titled *Erdős Rado Sunflower Theorem for Shifted Families*. The [June 1 v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02667v1) claimed the unrestricted conjecture. The latest theorem restricts to shifted families; its recurrence is f′(r,k)≤k^(2r) for r≤k−1 and f′(r,k)≤2f′(r−1,k) otherwise, with corollary f′(r,k)≤k^(2k−2)2^r. Definitions and the central shadow/shift argument were inspected. It does not transfer the conclusion to arbitrary families. Its threshold convention varies between abstract and introduction, so avoid an unqualified off-by-one numerical citation. This revision predates October 9.
- [Fukuyama, 2212.13609v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.13609v3), June 22, claims |F|>(ck⁴)^m forces a k-sunflower for rank≤m. The arXiv comment expressly says v3 fixes an error in v2. The v3 theorem and substantial proof text were inspected, but no complete validity audit or accepted independent proof confirmation was found in this bounded search. Later Bucić–He–Lee still describe the general conjecture as open. Consequently retain this as an unresolved proof claim; neither its title nor prior-version errors decide v3 validity.
- [Fukuyama, 2510.19037v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19037v2), December 1, 2025, claims threshold (ck² log m/log log m)^m for m,k>2. The latest arXiv comment says minor v1 errors were fixed. A retrieved primary-author [category archive](https://sites.psu.edu/sunflowerconjecture/category/mathematics/) contains a June 15, 2026 post saying the sublogarithmic proof remained incomplete and motivated the newer approach. Direct page fetch failed; this was search-indexed primary content. It is stronger contrary evidence than title-based dismissal, but is not an independently reconstructed counterexample. Exclude the claim from the established frontier.
- [Ge–Wang–Xu–Zhao, 2609.18995v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18995v1) states that VC dimension≤d and rank≤ℓ imply |H|≤Σ_{j=0}^ℓ(50dk)^j<2(50dk)^ℓ for k-sunflower-free H; the uniform threshold is (50dk)^ℓ. The abstract and HTML both give July 17, 2026 despite the September-style identifier. Preserve this metadata discrepancy. This is a bounded-VC result, not the unrestricted asymptotic conjecture. Rank 4 implies VC≤4, but substitution gives 600⁴=129,600,000,000 for k=3 and cannot improve the numerical rank-4 frontier. Statement and entropy/matching mechanism were inspected; complete proof audit was not undertaken.

No reliable expert statement resolving Fukuyama v3 was found in the bounded searches. Absence of such evidence is not proof of invalidity.
