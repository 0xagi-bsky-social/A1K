## Decision and precise comparison

The pre-existing research contract concerns finite simple exactly-four-uniform families on arbitrary support, forbidding three distinct sets with identical pairwise intersections, including the empty core. Its parameter M(4,3) is a maximum family size, not a forcing threshold. The local ordinary computer-assisted result claims M(4,3) <= 67; the corresponding forcing threshold is 68.

The strongest directly inspected comparable public preprint still gives M(4,3) <= 68. No retrieved primary source states the same or a stronger arbitrary-support rank-four upper bound. No concrete competing theorem or correction was located that defeats this comparison. The fresh evidence therefore supports resuming review under the already fixed, bounded known-frontier criterion. It does not certify exhaustive global priority, prove the manuscript correct, or authorize claims about solving the general Erdős–Rado conjecture. Final acceptance and local publication still require the remaining actual reviews and audits.
